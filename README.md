# Startup Customer Lab

Two tools for founders, built by Luigi Lugmayr from his Startup Marketing Playbook. Luigi has worked in marketing and technology for more than 30 years and advises startups in the US, the UK and the German-speaking countries (DACH).

## Start here

After installing, write one of these two sentences to Claude:

- "Simulate customers for my product: [what it is], [price], for [who], in [US, UK or DACH]."
- "Run a market entry check: we sell [product] in [home market] and want to enter [US, UK or DACH]."

Or give Claude your website address and it reads the facts from there. Started without any details, each tool explains itself in three lines and gives you a sentence to copy.

## What you get

**Customer simulation.** Describe your offer, your price and who it is for. Claude runs a panel of six sceptical simulated customers from three segments through the same six questions and gives you a report: who would try, who would pay and who would not, the strongest quotes for and against, the forces that move or hold these people, ranked hypotheses, and an invitation plus interview guide for real customer conversations. Use it to sharpen an idea, a message or a price before you spend time or money on the wrong customer.

**Market entry check.** Name your product and the market you want to enter: the US, the UK or DACH. Claude returns what usually breaks when a startup carries its home-market habits into that market, and how people there find, judge and pay for products like yours: outreach rules, language, trials and subscriptions, payment methods, channels and sales cycles. Every figure names its source.

Both tools answer in English or German.

## Your startup profile

Both tools keep a short profile of your company (offer, price, customers, home market, website) so you explain it only once. In apps where Claude works in a folder of yours (Claude Code, Cowork) it is saved there as `startup-profile.md`. In a normal chat it appears at the end of the report, and you can paste it into the next chat.

## What it is not

A simulation produces hypotheses, not proof. Simulated people agree too easily, so only real customers count. The market entry check is sourced orientation as of November 2026, not legal or tax advice.

## What the plugin runs, sends or fetches

Nothing runs in the background. The plugin contains two skills with text files only: no scripts, no hooks, no connectors, and it sends no data anywhere. The only file it writes is `startup-profile.md` in your own working folder, and only in apps where Claude can write files there. When you give Claude a website address for your own product, Claude may read that page if your app has web access switched on. Reports contain links to public sources and one link to book a free first call with Luigi at i4u.consulting.
