# United Kingdom

## What breaks on entry

Playbooks travel badly. These are the habits that break when a startup takes its home-market approach into the next market.

- US drip pricing and review gating, banned since April 2025.
- German checkout logic. The UK pays by card and wallet.
- Treating the UK as part of the EU. It has its own rules, with new subscription rules due in 2027.
- German caution with email. In the UK you may email people at companies without prior consent.

## B2B: how this market buys

- **Verify the pain:** Ask the same way, with British customers. LinkedIn reaches 38% of online adults (Ofcom 2025). Verify the pain with British customers, not by analogy to the US.
- **Position: wedge and vision:** You get into the conversation earlier. British and Irish buyers make first contact at 57% of the journey (6sense and MarketOne 2025, 754 buyers). That leaves more room to shape the requirements, so lead with a point of view on the problem.
- **Offer: free trial, clear path to paid:** Paid usually means an invoice on 30 days. 68% of British B2B sales are made on credit, with terms of about 30 days, and about two thirds of firms report late payments (Atradius 2026). Plan the gap between "converted" and "paid".
- **Decide how you sell:** Good data exists only for large deals. The UK and Ireland study covers purchases averaging £320,000 with ten or more people involved. Buyers there contact vendors after 57% of their journey, against 61% worldwide (6sense and MarketOne 2025, 754 buyers). For smaller deals there is no solid UK figure, so measure your own first ten sales.
- **First customers through networks:** Cold email to companies is allowed. You may email people at companies without prior consent if you say who you are and respect every opt-out. Sole traders are protected like consumers, and call lists must be screened against the TPS and CTPS registers (ICO). In Cognism's call data the UK ranks behind continental Europe and the US. LinkedIn reaches 38% of online adults (Ofcom 2025).
- **Channel mix: AI, founder, networks:** Higher reach on every platform. YouTube 94%, Instagram 78%, TikTok 56%, LinkedIn 38% of online adults (Ofcom 2025). English content carries over, the proof and examples should be British.
- **Scale what works:** Use the earlier conversation. Buyers make contact at 57% of their journey (6sense and MarketOne 2025). Sales people who bring a point of view get more out of that head start.

## B2C: how this market buys

- **Verify the pain:** Same language, separate rules. Verify with British customers. Do not assume US results carry over. Prolific is strongly represented in the UK and the US.
- **Build a love brand:** Word of mouth travels in private. WhatsApp reaches 90% of online adults, 76% daily (Ofcom 2025). Make the brand easy to pass on in a chat.
- **Offer: free trial, clear path to paid:** Show the full price. Hidden mandatory fees are banned since April 2025. New subscription rules with reminders and a cooling-off period after trials are announced for spring 2027.
- **First 1,000 by hand, then evangelists:** A WhatsApp community reaches almost everyone. WhatsApp 90%, YouTube 94%, Facebook 93%, Instagram 78%, TikTok 56% of online adults (Ofcom 2025).
- **Prove organic growth:** Cards, wallets and honest reviews. 22% of adults used buy-now-pay-later (UK Finance 2026). Fake reviews are banned since April 2025.
- **Channel mix: AI, founder, where people shop:** Marketplaces reach nearly everyone. Monthly reach among online adults: Amazon 88%, eBay 63%, Temu 58%, Vinted 29% (Ofcom 2025). TikTok Shop since 2021.
- **Amplify: channels, incentives, community:** Reward the purchase, never the review. Fake and gated reviews are banned since April 2025. 22% of adults used buy-now-pay-later (UK Finance 2026).
